Rose Infused

Black Rose Hair Oil is our premium formula, inspired by the ancient use of rose oil in royal hair care traditions. Black rose extract is exceptionally rare and rich in polyphenols and antioxidants that protect hair from environmental damage and free radicals.

Unlike synthetic rose-scented oils that use artificial fragrance, our Black Rose Oil uses actual rose extract, giving you both the therapeutic benefits and the genuine, subtle fragrance of fresh roses that lasts all day.

Perfect for all hair types, especially recommended for hair that is prone to breakage, lacks shine or needs a luxurious treatment.

  • Prevents premature hair whitening / greying
  • Eliminates dandruff from roots
  • Strengthens hair roots and follicles
  • Deeply conditions and removes dryness
  • Promotes new hair growth
  • Adds shine and volume to lifeless hair
  • Strengthens weak and brittle hair

Complete 45-day Treatment Plan

10

Phase 1 | Black Rose Oil (10 Nights)

Apply Black Rose Oil to hair roots every night for 10 consecutive nights. Wash hair with Nasir Herbal Shampoo in the morning.

5

Phase 2 | Golden Oil (5 Days)

Apply Golden Oil to hair roots and massage gently into the scalp. Leave overnight and wash with Nasir Herbal Shampoo the next morning.

5

Phase 3 | Alternating Oils (5 Days)

Continue alternating Black Rose Oil and Golden Oil daily. Wash with Nasir Herbal Shampoo each time.

Note: After 45 days, alternate Black Rose Oil and Golden Oil in 5-day cycles. Continue for 4045 days for full results.
